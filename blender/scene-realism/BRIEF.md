# Reference analysis and production brief

Target is the original generated concept at reference/target.png, 1536×1024. Rebuild warm editorial desk for real-time Three.js, metres, <=250k triangles where possible, <=10 MB initial payload. Static props; camera navigation belongs to runtime. Preserve the former .blend and original assets.

Composition: almost frontal elevated view, slight view from right; desk front edge about 73% of image height, monitor centered (roughly x=.31–.72, y=.12–.39), laptop x=.34–.60 y=.34–.64, car x=.02–.28 y=.36–.61, phone x=.84–.98 y=.56–.71, printer x=.79–.96 y=.27–.43. Foreground chair occludes underside only. Typography occupies upper left; desk carries a dense but readable arrangement.

Main silhouette corrections: lower camera pitch, flatter monitor, larger laptop and car relative to desk; introduce cabinet left and chair foreground. Car must have panel thickness, layered aero and wheel/suspension detail rather than merely repainting red surfaces. A5 open sketchbook right of laptop, letter sheet between book and phone, paper at printer rear.

Material palette: warm greige desk #d8d3ca, charcoal wall #1d1c1b, cream #ece6da, oxblood #6e0d0d, black carbon car, silver anodized laptop. Large warm key upper-left/front, weak cool fill, dark environment, warm wall pool. Baked static diffuse lighting and contact shadows, restrained real-time reflections. No brand logos or watermark; text/racing decals original and generic.

Source skills: blender-director/reference-image-match, realistic-style, materials, lighting, vehicle-artist. Figma creates original wireframe ship screen, sketchbook/resume artwork, and editorial overlay. Geometry blockout can progress while the Figma team selection is pending; final textures are dependent on the Figma phase.
